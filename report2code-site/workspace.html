Sample Note: Inpatient H&P & Progress Summary | Attending: Dr. Marcus Reed, MD (Internal Medicine)

CHIEF COMPLAINT: 64-year-old female presenting for routine inpatient optimization and worsening peripheral edema.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: Patient has a longstanding history of poorly controlled Type 2 diabetes with severe peripheral polyneuropathy. Recent lab evaluation demonstrates persistent renal deterioration consistent with Stage 3 CKD (eGFR 42 mL/min/1.73m², elevated microalbumin/creatinine ratio 340 mcg/mg). Patient is also undergoing medical management for essential systemic hypertension on lisinopril 20 mg daily.

REVIEW OF SYSTEMS / PULMONARY: Patient endorsed intermittent wheezing, dyspnea on exertion, and mild productive cough over the last 3 days, consistent with acute exacerbation of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

IMPRESSION & CODING INSTRUCTIONS: Documented diabetic kidney disease. Linkage between type 2 diabetes mellitus and chronic kidney disease confirmed. Hypertension managed medically. Acute bronchitis ruled out.
