Support

Answers to common questions, and a way to report a wrong code.

How do I get codes from a note?

Open Workspace, paste the note and press Analyze Note. Accept each code you agree with.

What does Check mean?

The code needs your review, for example the doctor has not linked two conditions. Read the note beside the code.

Why was a condition not coded?

Negated conditions (such as denies fever) and past ones (such as history of) are not coded as active diagnoses.

Why are there no CKD stage codes?

WHO ICD-10 has no stage-wise CKD codes. Those belong to the US ICD-10-CM.

What is a dagger and asterisk code?

A pair of codes for one condition. The dagger names the cause and the asterisk names the body part affected. Code the dagger first.

Can I use these codes for claims?

Only after a certified coder verifies them. Report2Code is a practice and support tool.
