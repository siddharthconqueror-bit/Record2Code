About Report2Code

Report2Code helps coders in Indian hospitals turn a doctor's note into ICD-10 codes. It works from the WHO ICD-10 book, not the US ICD-10-CM.

What it does

  • Suggests codes and shows the words it used.
  • Skips negated and past conditions.
  • Shows book notes, dagger and asterisk pairs.
  • Tells you when to query the doctor.

What it does not do

  • Decide a final code for you.
  • Guess a diagnosis the note does not state.
  • Replace a certified coder.

One condition, two codes

Some conditions need a dagger code (†) for the cause and an asterisk code (*) for the body part affected.
